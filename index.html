The Ambassador Effect

I started with a simple bet: the most convincing voice for any brand isn't a paid ad, it's the people who already believe in it. So I built a program to find those people, coach them, and give them a real reason to speak up. Not as recruiters, but as genuine champions for the product, culture, and workplace they were part of.

That program grew into a channel that outperformed nearly everything else in the mix. Trust gets built person by person, long before it ever shows up in a campaign brief.

Careers, Reimagined

I led the strategy behind rebuilding the careers site from the ground up, partnering closely with marketing and creative to turn a single page into a real destination. The win was never the page count. It was clarity: a more streamlined, informative experience built on real employee stories, video, and a messaging framework people could actually picture themselves inside.

That same EVP work became the backbone for messaging well beyond the site itself, lifting brand awareness 45% across key markets and setting the foundation every other piece of content and communication built on.
